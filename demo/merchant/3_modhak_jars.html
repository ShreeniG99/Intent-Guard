Product description

The Modhak Modustack range is a set of six clear glass jars with brushed stainless-steel lids, made to keep a dry-goods pantry organised. Each jar holds 800 ml, and the gasket inside the screw lid seals tightly enough to keep atta and rava dry through a humid monsoon.

The lid and base are flat so a stack of three sits stable on a shelf or inside a drawer instead of toppling. The glass is clear enough to read contents at a glance and, unlike plastic, doesn't stain or hold odour from spices. A wide mouth makes filling and scooping easy, and one measuring spoon is included with the set.

Wash by hand with warm water; the lid gasket is not dishwasher safe. Jars are refrigerator safe for storing prepped ingredients — avoid direct flame, oven and microwave.
