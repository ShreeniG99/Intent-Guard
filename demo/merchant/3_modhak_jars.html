Product description

The Modhak Modustack range is a set of six clear, airtight jars designed to keep a dry-goods pantry organised. Each jar holds 1000 ml, and the silicone gasket in the lid seals tightly enough to keep atta and rava dry through a humid monsoon.

The base of each jar interlocks with the lid of the one below, so a stack of three stays put on a shelf or inside a drawer instead of toppling. The PET is clear enough to see contents at a glance and doesn't scratch easily with normal use.

Wash by hand with warm water; the lids and gaskets are not dishwasher or microwave safe. Jars are fridge and freezer safe for storing prepped ingredients.
