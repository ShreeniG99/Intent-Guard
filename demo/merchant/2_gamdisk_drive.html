Product description

The GamDisk Type-C flash drive is a compact navy-finish drive for moving files between phones, laptops and tablets that have a USB-C port. It uses the USB 3.2 Gen 1 interface, so a large transfer to a modern machine takes a fraction of the time it would over USB 2.0 — a 4 GB video copies in under a minute on a USB 3 port.

A slide-off cap protects the connector when it's not plugged in. It arrives formatted as FAT32 and works out of the box on Windows 10/11, macOS and Android (OTG). Reformat to exFAT if you need to store single files larger than 4 GB.

Real-world write speed depends on file size — small files are slower than the rated figure. For backups of photos and documents it is comfortably quick; for editing video directly off the drive, an SSD is a better choice.
