Product description

DermShield Ultra Matte is a daily broad-spectrum sunscreen formulated for oily and combination skin in Indian conditions. The lightweight fluid absorbs in seconds and dries down to a true matte finish, so it sits well under makeup without pilling.

4% niacinamide works alongside the UV filters to help reduce the look of uneven tone and post-acne marks over time, while vitamin E and panthenol support the skin barrier. The filter system is tuned to avoid the grey-white cast that most SPF 50 sunscreens leave on deeper skin tones.

Apply two finger-lengths to the face and neck as the last step of your morning routine, 15 minutes before sun exposure. Reapply every 3–4 hours when outdoors. For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes.
